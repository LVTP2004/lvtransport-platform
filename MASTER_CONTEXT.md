# MASTER CONTEXT — LVTransport Operational Governance

LVTransport is a **truthful operational transport system**.

## Governing Philosophy
- Local-first persistence and operations.
- Operator-supervised execution.
- Audit-safe actions and traceability.
- Recovery-oriented decisions over speed-only decisions.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries
LVTransport must never drift into:
- fake operational state,
- fake realtime systems,
- fake analytics,
- autonomous destructive AI,
- hidden recovery execution,
- distributed complexity without necessity.

## Operational Governance Intent
This documentation set standardizes:
- startup validation,
- backup and restore expectations,
- integrity and corruption handling,
- recovery replay controls,
- incident response,
- migration rollout safety.

Use runbooks in `/runbooks` as source of truth for human and AI-assisted operations.
