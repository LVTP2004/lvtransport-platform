# BACKUP ARCHAEOLOGY LAW V1

Status: ACTIVE
Created: 2026-06-04T12:56:09+02:00

## Purpose

Prevent backup files from becoming operational noise.

## Law

Backup files are evidence until classified.

No backup file may be deleted, promoted, or ignored until Atlas classifies it.

## Classifications

PROTECTED:
Must preserve. Contains important recovery or decision history.

HISTORICAL:
Keep as institutional history.

TEMPORARY:
Can be removed after validation.

CANDIDATE_DELETE:
Can be deleted after Founder approval.

UNKNOWN:
Requires Atlas review.

## Applies To

- .bak
- .bak.*
- .safe-autopatch
- forge rollback backups
- pre-root-fix files
- temporary repair artifacts
