# Continuity Summarization Policy

## Supported deterministic summaries
- Ride continuity summary
- Recovery/replay continuity summary
- Incident continuity summary
- Migration continuity summary
- General correlation_id summary

## Rule-based constraints
Summaries are generated by deterministic rules over indexed operational memory records only.

The system:
- sequences observed events
- reports known continuity and lineage
- identifies missing fields/data
- recommends inspection steps

The system never:
- invents causes, actors, transitions, outcomes, or missing events
- infers root cause without explicit indexed evidence
- simulates realtime state
- mutates operational state or executes replay
- calls external AI/cloud services

## Missing-data policy
If matching records are absent or incomplete, summaries explicitly mark `status` as `missing` or `partial` and enumerate gaps in `missingData`.

## Operator supervision
Continuity outputs are decision support only. Operators must validate lineage in source systems before taking remediation actions.
