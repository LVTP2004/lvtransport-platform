# LVTransport — Product Vision

## UX Philosophy

LVTransport must feel:
- calm
- premium
- operational
- trustworthy
- understated
- intelligent

Never:
- flashy
- cyberpunk
- over-animated
- dashboard-heavy
- startup-generic

---

# Visual Identity

## Colors
- graphite
- warm gold
- subtle glass effects

## Style
- premium Belgian transport
- executive operational calm
- minimalistic
- operationally serious

---

# MoniRide Philosophy

MoniRide is:
- operational assistance
- calm tracking
- customer reassurance

MoniRide is NOT:
- gimmick AI
- fake assistant
- chat spam
- noisy interface

---

# Operational Honesty

NEVER:
- fake tracking
- fake ETA
- fake GPS
- fake payment states
- fake analytics
- fake operational continuity

If data is unavailable:
- show honest empty states
- show recovery guidance
- show contact escalation

---

# Founder-Controlled UX

Codex and AI systems must preserve:
- approved UX flows
- approved architecture
- approved interaction patterns
- approved visual identity

No redesigns unless explicitly requested.
