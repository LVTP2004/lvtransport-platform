# Customer Runtime Narrative Alignment

## Objective
Align customer-facing language to LVTransport as **operational mobility infrastructure** with a calm, premium, low-noise tone.

## Narrative Guardrails
- Keep graphite/gold premium visual identity and restrained interaction density.
- Prefer continuity, reliability, and deterministic clarity over promotional language.
- Explain benefits in operational terms: predictable pickup, verified status, bounded updates.
- Preserve simplicity: short labels, clear actions, no telemetry overload.

## Canonical Messaging
Use:
- Operational mobility infrastructure
- Continuity-aware transport
- Operational reliability
- Deterministic premium mobility

Avoid:
- Dashboard/operator jargon in customer flows
- AI theater or speculative capability claims
- High-noise status copy

## Runtime Surfaces
- Booking confirmation: immutable reference + verified timestamp.
- Pre-ride state: continuity indicators (driver verified, vehicle verified, route synchronized).
- In-ride state: bounded realtime progress and deterministic ETA windows.
- Post-ride state: transparent invoice lineage and support accountability link.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Customer copy is operationally calm and premium.
- No “AI automation” claims in customer UX.
- Every status statement maps to verifiable operational state.

## UI Tone Contract
- Premium graphite/gold visual hierarchy remains unchanged.
- Messaging emphasizes certainty, timestamps, and verified status.
- Escalation language remains calm and accountable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Booking, pricing, and ride-state copy explicitly references verification and continuity when applicable.
