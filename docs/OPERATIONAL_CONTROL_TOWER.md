# Operational Control Tower

## Objective
Deliver calm operational visibility with bounded realtime and replay-aware supervision.

## Surfaces
- Continuity views per runtime (customer/driver/ops/governance)
- Bounded realtime maps (rate-limited and evidence-backed)
- Replay visibility for incidents and transition audits
- Synchronization health indicators
- Incident surfaces with severity and ownership

## UX Constraints
- Restrained telemetry rendering.
- Low-noise operational focus.
- No observability chaos or vanity dashboards.
